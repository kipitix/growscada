# Infrastructure Layer (`internal/server/infrastructure/`)

> Architecture reference for the GrowSCADA server. `AGENTS.md` links here: read this
> document before changing the layer it describes. Decisions are in `docs/adr/`; the
> vocabulary is in `GLOSSARY.md`.

- **PostgreSQL repositories** implement domain repository interfaces using `database/sql` + `lib/pq`.
- **Migrations** live in `internal/server/infrastructure/postgres/migrations/` and are managed with [Goose](https://github.com/pressly/goose): one database, three schemas, one chain each (ADR 0011) — `public/` (the server's: organizations, projects with their deleted mark and description, outbox, policy_jobs; goose table `goose_db_version`; `20261105000001_drop_project_discard` removed `projects.discarding`, `20261112000000_project_description` added `projects.description`, `20261113000000_user_settings` added `public.user_settings` — one person's settings per Organization and key, with a jsonb payload and a Version, `user_id` without a foreign key until Users exist (ADR 0024)), `engineering/` (Libraries with their description, WidgetTypes, Labels and their assignments (`labels`, `commit_labels`; the starter `ready_for_import`/`ready_for_deploy` are ordinary rows, ADR 0025), Library Imports (`library_imports`, each naming a saved state), Scenes, Widgets, Tag definitions, Devices, Environments with the parameters of a pair, and the Drafts with their DraftChanges and saved states; `engineering.goose_db_version`; `20261105000001_drafts` added `engineering.drafts` and `engineering.draft_changes`, dropped `engineering.edit_locks` and the per-entity `version` columns, ADR 0016, ADR 0017; `20261105000002_library_draft` dropped `engineering.widget_types.version` and `libraries.discarding_to`; `20261027000000_libraries` put the WidgetTypes into the Library "Main"; `20261101000000_devices_and_environments` added the Devices, the Environments, `tags.device_id` and the index of the pairs by Device; `20261103000000_project_files` and `20261104000000_drop_project_files` added and removed the copy of the last ProjectFile a Deploy sent (ADR 0014); `20261105000000_edit_locks` and `20261106000000_remove_edit_lock` added and removed the editing locks (ADR 0022); `20261108000000_commits` added `engineering.commits` (a Draft's numbered snapshot with its `content` jsonb and `draft_version`), `engineering.commit_library_pins` (renamed to `commit_library_imports` by) and the Draft's `last_commit_number`/`last_commit_draft_version` (ADR 0021); `20261112000000_library_description` added `libraries.description` ; `20261113000000_draft_clean_version` added `engineering.drafts.clean_version` ; `20261114000000_labels` and `20261114000001_commit_labels` added the Labels, the assignments and the two starter labels, and `20261114000002_library_imports` rewrote `library_pins` into `library_imports` (a `commit_id` and its number), `commit_library_pins` into `commit_library_imports`, the stored documents and the Draft changes, deleted the legacy Releases with their imports and Widgets and cleared every stack, `runtime/` (the copies of the Tag definitions, Devices and Environment Devices — the pairs with foreign keys to both copies, cascading, and the project foreign keys of the copies cascading —, the Tag states apart from them in `runtime.tag_states`, `runtime.device_tokens`, the Deployments in `runtime.deployments`, the Checkpoints in `runtime.checkpoints` with the index of their positions by Project, and the Journal in `runtime.journal` with its sequence `runtime.journal_seq` and the subscribers' positions in `runtime.journal_cursors`; `runtime.goose_db_version`; `20261101000000_tag_states_and_devices` split the state from the definition; `20261103000001_revisions` added the Deployments; `20261103000002_deferrable_environment_names` made the uniqueness of an Environment's name checkable at a commit; `20261104000000_revision_cause` added a Deployment's `cause` and the `rolled_back_from` a rollback copies; `20261105000001_revision_cause_file` widened the `cause` check to `file` (ADR 0018); `20261106000000_journal` added `runtime.journal`, its sequence, `runtime.tag_states.last_write_at` and `runtime.journal_cursors` (ADR 0019); `20261107000000_deployments` renamed `runtime.revisions` to `runtime.deployments` and the Journal's `revision` entry type to `deployment` ; `20261111000000_checkpoints` added `runtime.checkpoints` (ADR 0023); `20261114000002_deployment_commit_number` replaced `release_number` with `commit_number` and rewrote the Journal entries and the Checkpoints ; `20261115000000_drop_releases` drops the Release tables — it lives in the runtime chain because the 85_4 migration still reads them, and the Engineering chain is applied first, applied in that order; the public migration `20261026000000` moved the tables and marks the baseline of the two others. Go code applies them with `migrations.Up` (embedded); the debug `docker-compose.yaml` applies the three chains (a goose service each: the `goose-docker` image runs `goose -dir=/migrations up` whatever the command, the table goes in `GOOSE_EXTRA_ARGS`) and the seed data (`test_data/`, a chain on `goose_db_version`) on `make db_up`. Queries name the role schemas (`engineering.tags`, `runtime.tags`); `public` stays unqualified.
- **Repositories of a Project's content** (`NewProjectScopes`, `NewTagRepositoryPostgres(db, project)`, `NewSceneRepositoryPostgres(db, project)`, `NewLibraryImportRepositoryPostgres(db, project)`, `NewDeviceRepositoryPostgres(db, project)`, `NewEnvironmentRepositoryPostgres(db, project)`, `NewEnvironmentDeviceRepositoryPostgres(db, project)`) are read repositories of that Project (a pair has no ID of its own: it is keyed by `(environment_id, device_id)`); those of a Library's Draft (`NewWidgetTypeRepositories`, `NewWidgetTypeRepositoryPostgres(db, library)`) the Library and are reads only, its writes going through the Draft's repository; `FindSoleOrganization` reads the one Organization the migrations create (`main.go` fails to start without exactly one).
- **The Runtime node's repository** (`postgres/runtime.NewTagStateRepositoryPostgres`) over `runtime.tags` (the copy of a Tag definition, its supplying Device in `device_id`) and `runtime.tag_states` (its state, with `last_write_at`), joined on every read; a write locks the state's row, checks the writer against `device_id` and compares the value and Quality, appending a `tag_state` entry to `runtime.journal` (position `seq` from `runtime.journal_seq`) in the same transaction only when the state changes; it reads `public.projects`, never `engineering.*`. `postgres/runtime/journal_codec.go` encodes and decodes `recordv0.Entry` rows (the payload by `type`, `schema_version` as a string). `postgres/runtime.NewDeviceTokenRepositoryPostgres` keeps `runtime.device_tokens` (the token's hash, fingerprint, Device and that Device's Project) for the Runtime node's `DeviceTokenService`. `postgres/runtime.NewDeploymentRepositoryPostgres` keeps `runtime.deployments` (the numbered ProjectFile: what the Project runs, with the `cause` of a deploy, a rollback or a project file, the `rolled_back_from` a rollback copied and the `commit_number` of the saved state it came from, nil for a file deploy) appends a `deployment` entry to `runtime.journal` (the number, the cause and the copied Deployment, never the document) in the transaction that numbers it, and rebuilds the copies of the Tag definitions, the Devices, the Environments and their pairs from the document by diff, so the Tag states and the Device Tokens survive; `CreateDeployment` (which takes the cause and the saved state's number) and `Rollback` share the advisory lock, the numbering and that diff, and `FindAll`/`FindByNumber` refuse a Project that is not live; both insert the Deployment's Checkpoint at the `deployment` entry's position after the copies are rebuilt, in the same transaction (ADR 0023). `postgres/runtime.NewCheckpointRepositoryPostgres` keeps `runtime.checkpoints` (`(project_id, seq)` and the record's payload): `Nearest` reads the latest at or before a position (refusing a Project that is not live), `DueProjects` names the live Projects whose position moved far enough past their latest one, and `Snapshot` takes one periodic snapshot in a `REPEATABLE READ` transaction with `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`; `checkpoint_codec.go` encodes and decodes the record. The Engineering node keeps no copy (ADR 0014).
- **The Drafts** (`postgres/repositories/draft_repository_postgres.go`): `engineering.drafts` and `engineering.draft_changes` — `Read`, `Update` (locks the Draft's row, checks the stated Version, runs a `draft.Session`, writes the touched rows from its snapshots, one DraftChange and the outbox, raises the Version and moves the stack), `Replace` (Discard and Revert: same, clearing the stack, no DraftChange; a `clean` replacement — its content is the last saved state's — makes the new Version the Clean Version, otherwise the marker stays), `Purge` (the cleanup of a deleted Project), `ClearStack` (no caller: a Deploy and a Save change no Draft and leave its stack), and `CheckVersion` (the Version check a multi-step act over the Draft makes; no caller — a Deploy no longer builds the ProjectFile, ADR 0022); the loader reads only the entities an operation touches (`draft_repository_snapshots.go`), and the snapshots are encoded by `postgres/draftchange`.
- **The Labels** (`postgres/repositories/label_repository_postgres.go`): `engineering.labels` and `engineering.commit_labels` — `FindAll` (by name ignoring case, with the number of assignments), `FindByID`, `Create` (a name another label holds ignoring case maps to `label.ErrLabelNameTaken`), `Update`, `Delete` (the assignments go by the foreign key) and the assignment writes: `Assign` and `Unassign` lock the label and the saved state and are idempotent, and `LabelsOfScope` reads the chips of a history in one query. The label's events (`label_created`, `label_updated`, `label_deleted`, `label_assigned`, `label_unassigned`) go through the outbox to the server's stream; no `runtime` table is read.

- **Outbox** (`postgres/outbox/`): `Append` (called by the repositories' shared `aggregateStore` template), the internal event codec (not a contract; the payload carries the `project_id` of a content event) and the `Dispatcher` (`Run`, `Notify` — wired to repositories via `repositories.NotifyOnCommit`, `DrainOnce` for deterministic tests; `WithPolicyRunner` makes it enqueue policy jobs) and the `PolicyRunner` (`Run` — woken by the dispatcher and by the timer of the next due job, `RunDueOnce` for deterministic tests); the policy registry is assembled in `main.go`. One dispatcher and one runner per database.
